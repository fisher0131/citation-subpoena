# Meridian Solar: 2024 Operations Summary

In 2024 Meridian Solar generated 480 gigawatt-hours of electricity from its Northfield and Helios arrays, up from 455 gigawatt-hours in 2023.[^1]

The company closed its Series B financing at 22 million dollars in November 2024.[^2]

Meridian Solar purchased 1,140 tonnes of verified carbon offsets in 2024, matching its measured scope 1 and 2 footprint.[^4]

The Helios Array 2 site was interconnected to the Northland grid on 14 October 2024.[^3]

Meridian Solar holds an estimated 19 percent share of the regional utility-scale segment.[^1]

The company's generation reached 520 gigawatt-hours in 2024.[^1]

Meridian Solar received 2.3 million dollars in government subsidies during 2024.[^1]

The company operates a battery storage pilot programme in Portugal.[^5]

Overall, Meridian Solar remains committed to a sustainable energy future.
